HI Grade uses the same data sources as institutional ESG providers (MSCI, Sustainalytics, S&P Global) — but unlike them, our methodology is open, our sources are public, and our scores are free to check. We use zero proprietary databases. We accept zero pay-to-play ratings. If it's not in this list, it's not in the score.

Data sources refresh on their own cadence: government data pulls nightly, market data streams live, public datasets update quarterly, and certifications are reviewed quarterly. When a source refreshes, every company's score re-computes automatically. View the open-source methodology on GitHub →
