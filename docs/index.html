About

Think human intelligence.

measures the balance between Human Intelligence and Artificial Intelligence at every company. We score organizations across five dimensions — the things AI can't be: conscious, empathetic, ethical, alive, and transparent.

Every company gets a HI Grade™ — a score from 0 to 100. Pass all 3 gates — score, balance, and integrity — and you're gold HI Grade — the highest standard.

📈 Dynamic Hybrid Scoring Model

The gold threshold isn't a fixed number. It's mean + 2 standard deviations of every scored company — recalculated quarterly. Companies force each other to improve. Two failsafes: a hard floor (never below 55) and a ratchet (can only go up, never down). The math decides, not us.

⚖ Balance Floor Rule

Every dimension must score ≥ 42 — the answer to life, the universe, and everything. Any dimension below 42? Not balanced. You can't claim balance when any dimension is failing.

🏅 The 3 Gates to Gold HI Grade

1
Score Gate — Composite score must meet or exceed the dynamic gold threshold (currently recalculated quarterly from the scoring population).
2
Balance Gate — All five HUMAN dimensions must score ≥ 42. No single dimension can be failing.
3
Integrity Gate — No Humanwashing™ flags and Algorithmic Harm Index below 30. You can't buy your way past this gate.

Data Sources (42)

HI Grades are computed from 42 free, public data sources across 24 sub-signals and 32 API endpoints: SEC EDGAR (10-K, 8-K, DEF 14A, Form 4), EPA, BLS, CDP, Glassdoor, DEI & HRC indices, Yahoo Finance, FMP, Alpha Vantage, Finnhub, FRED, NewsAPI (150K+ sources), Layoffs.fyi, WARN Act, CEO signals, CFPB, FEC, CPSC, Have I Been Pwned, iFixit, OSHA, FTC, EEOC, USPTO, FDA, DOL, BBB, GRI, SBTi, IRS 990, B Corp Directory, Charity Navigator, and industry deforestation risk.

All scores are estimated from public data. Gold HI Grade status is earned algorithmically, not purchased.

🛡 Data Integrity — 3-Layer Validation

Bad data in = bad scores out. Every pipeline run passes through 3 layers of automated validation before scores go live.

Layer 1 — Input: Every data point is checked for physical possibility before scoring. Negative headcounts, impossible Glassdoor ratings, out-of-range dimensions — caught and blocked.

Layer 2 — Output: After scoring, before publishing. Did any score move more than 15 points overnight? Is the distribution shape normal? Are known ethical leaders scoring suspiciously low? If anything looks wrong, the pipeline stops and waits for human review.

Layer 3 — MSSI: Maximum Single-Source Impact. No single data source — a Glassdoor scrape, a single SEC filing, a news article — can move any sub-signal by more than 15 points. Material changes require corroboration from 2+ independent sources. The data must agree, or the change doesn't publish.

🔒 Security

The API is read-only — no write endpoints exist. All user input is sanitized. Search queries are rate-limited to 30/min. CORS is whitelisted to thehibalance.org and the browser extension only. The extension runs zero AI, zero tracking, zero phone-home — your browsing stays private.

Open Source

🎭 Humanwashing™ Detection

Detects companies that perform human involvement without practicing it. Revenue-per-employee ratios, headcount drops while AI spend surges, disclosed AI usage that doesn't match detected tools. If the humans are gone but the marketing says they're still there — we catch it.

⚡ Algorithmic Harm Index™

Cross-cutting penalty for algorithms that divide, addict, or manipulate. Five factors: division, addiction, manipulation, transparency, and human override. Five factors measured across every brand. Your algorithm connects people? Prove it.

The Founder

started on Earth Day 2025 with a simple question: if AI is changing everything, who's measuring what we're losing?

I'd spent years in technology — enough to know what AI could do, and enough to worry about what it was replacing. Not just jobs. The craft behind the work. The empathy in the service. The humans in the loop.

I couldn't find a tool that measured any of that. So I built one. The methodology is open source because a transparency framework that hides its own math would be hypocritical. The name is because it's both a greeting and a question: is it Human Intelligence, or Artificial?

Don't panic. Every journey starts somewhere. The data will get better. The gates will get harder. The companies will adapt. That's the point.
