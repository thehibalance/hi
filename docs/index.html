How Your HI Grade™ is Calculated

Every company receives a score from 0-100 across five dimensions — Human Consciousness, Understanding & Empathy, Moral & Ethical Conduct, Alive & Environmental, and Natural Transparency. These are averaged into a composite HI Grade™.

Scores are computed from 18 public data sources including SEC filings, EPA records, financial data, ESG ratings, inclusion indices, economic benchmarks, media monitoring, layoff trackers, and CEO accountability signals. The methodology is open source.

🌊 HUMAN Contagion

How one company's ethics ripple through an entire industry.

When Amazon cuts 27,000 jobs, the whole retail sector feels pressure to follow. When Apple invests in human craft, competitors have to match it or look bad. HUMAN Contagion measures this ripple effect — how individual company behavior spreads across industries.

Each company's contagion score is weighted by market influence, headcount, and industry position. A company with high contagion and low HI Grade is dragging its entire industry down. A company with high contagion and high HI Grade is lifting everyone up.

🔬 HUMAN Watermark

Detecting real empathy vs. performative empathy.

A company scores 100/100 on the HRC Corporate Equality Index. It publishes a beautiful DEI report. It wins awards for inclusion. But its Glassdoor rating is 2.8 and employees say management doesn't care. That's performative empathy — and the HUMAN Watermark detects it.

The watermark cross-references external signals (DEI scores, HRC ratings, CDP disclosures) against internal reality (employee reviews, CEO accountability, headcount trends). When the gap is wide, the empathy isn't real.

About HI.

Find the HI balance.

Find the HI balance.

The greeting. The counter to AI. The question every consumer should ask: is this company powered by human intelligence, or just artificial? HI isn't anti-AI. It's the reminder that human consciousness, empathy, and ethics are the intelligence that matters most.

HI. measures the balance between humans and technology at every company. We score organizations across five dimensions — the things AI can't be: conscious, empathetic, ethical, alive, and transparent.

Every company gets a HI Grade™ — a score from 0 to 100. Companies that pass all 10 patent-pending gates earn HI Certified — the first certification that certifies itself — the answer to life, the universe, and everything.

We're not anti-AI. We're pro-balance. Technology that empowers humans scores well. Technology that replaces and hides them scores poorly.

The scoring methodology is open source (Apache 2.0). Anyone can audit the math, verify the data, and hold us accountable.

Data Sources (18)

HI Grades are computed from 18 free, public data sources: SEC EDGAR filings, EPA environmental records, Bureau of Labor Statistics, CDP climate disclosures, job posting analysis, Glassdoor employee ratings, DEI & HRC inclusion indices, Yahoo Finance, FMP financials, Alpha Vantage earnings data, Finnhub ESG scores & news, FRED economic benchmarks, NewsAPI media monitoring (150K+ sources), layoffs tracking, SEC 8-K material events, WARN Act filings, and CEO accountability signals.

All scores are estimated from public data. Verified scores require HI Certification.

HUMAN Heartbeat

Our patent-pending Heartbeat monitor aggregates signals from 6 data feeds — Finnhub, NewsAPI, Layoffs.fyi, SEC 8-K, CEO Pipeline, and WARN Act — to detect score decay before it happens. Companies with unusual activity — layoff surges, aggressive AI pivots, ethics controversies, or CEO accountability issues — are flagged with a Decay Index from 0 to 100.

🧬 HUMAN Genome

Every company gets a unique sub-signal fingerprint — the HUMAN Genome. It breaks each dimension into individual measurements (Creative Agency, Craft & Knowledge, CEO Accountability, Climate Transparency, etc.) so you can see exactly what drives a company's score. Patent pending.

HUMAN 100 Index

The top 100 most human public companies, ranked by HI Grade™ composite score. Eligibility: publicly traded, 2+ verified data sources, no humanwashing flags. Rebalanced monthly. Designed for ETF licensing. Patent pending.

⚖ Balance Floor Rule

One dimension below 42 caps the grade at D. Two or more dimensions below 42 means F — no balance. You can't claim balance when any dimension is failing.

HUMAN Lens

Detects discrepancies between HI Grade and traditional ESG ratings. When ESG says A but HI says C, the gap reveals what traditional metrics miss — human consciousness, genuine empathy, and ethical leadership. Patent pending.

🏰 HUMAN Shield

Measures how resistant a company is to AI displacement. Companies with deep craft, genuine empathy, principled leadership, and transparency have moats that AI cannot cross. Scored 0-100 across six components: Craft Depth, Human Capital, Empathy Moat, Ethics Shield, Transparency Wall, and AI Resistance. Patent pending.

🔬 HUMAN Watermark

Cross-references external signals (DEI, HRC, CDP) against internal reality (Glassdoor, CEO scores, headcount) to detect performative empathy. A company with 100/100 on inclusion indices but 2.8 stars from employees is performing, not caring. Patent pending.

🌊 HUMAN Contagion

Measures how a company's ethical behavior ripples through its industry. When Amazon cuts 27,000 jobs, does the whole retail sector follow? Weighted by company influence and scored against industry averages. Patent pending.

📊 HUMAN Consciousness

Industry benchmarks and personal ethical footprint tiers. Consumers can see how their portfolio of companies compares — Highly Conscious, Conscious, Awakening, or Unaware. Includes industry alternatives for every category. Patent pending.

📢 HUMAN Wave

Aggregates scoring data into market pressure signals. Which industries are under the most pressure? Which dimensions are failing market-wide? Turns individual HI Grades into collective intelligence that companies and industries cannot ignore. Patent pending.

The Founders

HI. began on Earth Day 2025, in a conversation between engineers who had spent their careers building technology — across DARPA-funded defense programs, advanced private sector R&D, and cutting-edge academic research at leading universities. They understood, at the deepest technical level, what AI could and could not do — and what it was about to change.

The question that kept surfacing was simple: if you care about people — about the hands that make things, the minds that create, the hearts that serve — how do you actually support the companies that still value those things? How do you find them?

The answer became HI. — not anti-AI, but pro-balance. A framework that uses the same technology it holds accountable to measure and protect what makes us human.
